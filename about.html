About Me

Environmental scientist · GIS · Support Analyst at Esri · San Diego, CA

Professional Summary

I am an environmental scientist with a background in biology (BS, University of Washington 2024) and advanced skills in GIS, Python, and R. I currently work as a Support Analyst at Esri, helping government and enterprise GIS users get the most out of Esri products, and I bring experience in both geospatial research and customer service in technical and non-technical settings. Through collaborations with governmental (NASA, Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife) and non-profit (San Diego Tracking Team, Abrahms Lab UW) partners, I've developed skills in research biology and geospatial analysis. Above all, I'm passionate about applying emerging technologies like GIS and generative AI to support environmental decision-making and management.

Born and raised in the Imperial Valley and now based in San Diego, I have a deep connection to Southern California's environmental challenges and history. I'm expanding my technical expertise through advanced GIS certification while working on personal and professional projects focused on water resources, conservation, and environmental monitoring.

Selected Projects

Salton Sea Monitoring

Personal portfolio project
Built a Python and ArcGIS workflow that turns 41 years of Landsat imagery (1984–2025, Landsat 5, 8 and 9) into NDWI rasters and an animated time series. In my classification, the Sea's surface area fell about 20% between 1984 and 2025, from roughly 369 to 295 square miles. Published as an ArcGIS StoryMap.

Puerto Rico Land Cover & Bioclimate

NASA DEVELOP, with WildMon
Interpolated eight bioclimatic variables at 30 m resolution with Empirical Bayesian Kriging, then classified 17 land cover classes across more than 8,565 km² using PCA and a Self-Organizing Map. Delivered to WildMon for species distribution work. See the StoryMap.

Riparian Vegetation Analysis, South Africa

NASA DEVELOP
