About Me

Environmental scientist · GIS · San Diego, CA

Professional Summary

I am an environmental scientist with a background in biology (BS, University of Washington 2024) and advanced skills in GIS, Python, and R. Through collaborations with governmental (NASA, Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife) and non-profit (San Diego Tracking Team, Abrahms Lab UW) partners, I've developed skills in research biology and geospatial analysis. Above all, I'm passionate about applying emerging technologies like GIS and generative AI to support environmental decision-making and management.

Born and raised in the Imperial Valley and now based in San Diego, I have a deep connection to Southern California's environmental challenges and history. I'm expanding my technical expertise through advanced GIS certification while working on personal and professional projects focused on water resources, conservation, and environmental monitoring.
